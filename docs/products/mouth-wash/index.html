Face Dent Mouth Wash

Complete your oral care routine with our refreshing mouth wash. Fights bacteria, freshens breath, and promotes gum health.

Key Benefits

  • Kills 99.9% of bacteria
  • Long-lasting fresh breath
  • Strengthens enamel
  • Reduces plaque

How to Use

Use twice daily after brushing. Pour 20ml (4 teaspoons) into cup. Rinse for 30 seconds and spit out. Do not swallow.
