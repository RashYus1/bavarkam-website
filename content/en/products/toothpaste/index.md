## BavarKam Toothpaste

Start your day with our fluoride toothpaste that provides complete protection against cavities, plaque, and bad breath.

### Key Benefits

- Cavity protection
- Plaque prevention
- Fresh breath
- Enamel strengthening
- Gentle whitening

### How to Use

Brush twice daily for 2 minutes. Use pea-sized amount. Spit out, do not swallow. For best results, use with BavarKam toothbrush.
