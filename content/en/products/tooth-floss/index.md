## Face Dent Dental Floss

Reach where your toothbrush can't with our high-quality dental floss. Essential for complete oral hygiene.

### Key Benefits

- Removes plaque between teeth
- Prevents gum disease
- Fresh mint flavor
- Glides easily between teeth
- Strong and shred-resistant

### How to Use

Use 18 inches of floss. Wind around fingers and gently slide between teeth. Curve against tooth and scrape plaque away.
