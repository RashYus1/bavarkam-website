## BavarKam Hand Cream

Keep your hands soft, smooth, and protected with our nourishing hand cream. Perfect for daily use, especially in dry conditions.

### Key Benefits

- Intensive moisture
- Quick absorption
- Non-greasy formula
- Protects from dryness
- Long-lasting softness

### How to Use

Apply small amount to hands and massage until fully absorbed. Reapply as needed, especially after washing hands.

### Ingredients

Aqua, Glycerin, Shea Butter, Almond Oil, Vitamin E, Panthenol, Allantoin, Natural Fragrance
