## Face Dent Toothbrush

Experience superior cleaning with our ergonomically designed toothbrush. Perfect for daily oral care.

### Key Features

- Soft bristles for gentle cleaning
- Ergonomic non-slip handle
- Multi-action bristle arrangement
- Reaches back teeth easily
- BPA-free materials

### Bristle Types

- **Outer bristles**: Longer for reaching between teeth
- **Inner bristles**: Shorter for surface cleaning
- **Tip bristles**: For back teeth cleaning

### Usage Tips

Replace every 3 months or after illness. Rinse thoroughly after use. Store in upright position to dry.
