## Face Dent Face Wash

Experience the perfect start to your day with our refreshing face wash. Gently cleanses while maintaining your skin's natural moisture balance.

### Key Benefits

- Deep pore cleansing
- Removes impurities and excess oil
- Maintains skin pH balance
- Refreshing and cooling effect
- Suitable for daily use

### How to Use

Wet face with lukewarm water. Apply small amount and massage gently. Rinse thoroughly and pat dry. Use morning and evening.
